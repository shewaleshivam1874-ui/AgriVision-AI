About AgriVision AI

AgriVision AI is an intelligent agricultural crop-health assistance system designed for early crop disease identification, visual pathology explanation, and structured disease management support.

CORE OBJECTIVE

Our Mission

To empower farmers, agronomists, and agricultural extension officers with transparent, accessible, and explainable AI insights to identify foliage diseases early, preserve crop yields, and reduce unnecessary chemical pesticide overuse.

🧠

How AI Helps Agriculture

Computer vision models analyze visual leaf foliage symptoms—such as chlorosis, target-ring spot lesions, fungal spores, and bacterial spots—identifying early pathology stages before extensive crop damage spreads.

💡

Explainable AI (XAI)

Rather than operating as an opaque "black box", AgriVision AI incorporates Explainable AI (Grad-CAM feature heatmaps and visual symptom explanations) to highlight exact leaf regions that influenced the model's prediction.

Important AI Limitations

AgriVision AI is designed as an agricultural decision-support tool. Users should understand the following limitations:

  • AI diagnostics rely on visible leaf foliage symptoms and photograph quality.
  • Different plant diseases can display visually similar chlorosis or leaf spot lesions.
  • Environmental sunburn or physical wind damage can resemble biological disease spots.
  • A leaf photo cannot measure exact soil pH, nitrogen/phosphorus levels, or vascular root rot.
  • AI assessments assist—and do not replace—qualified agronomists or laboratory diagnostic testing.
