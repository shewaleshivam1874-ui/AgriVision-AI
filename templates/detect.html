💡 Tips for Better Results (Click to expand/collapse)
  • Use a clear, well-lit image of a single crop leaf.
  • Keep the leaf properly focused to avoid excessive blur.
  • Ensure sufficient natural lighting without extreme glare or dark shadows.
  • Keep the affected diseased region clearly visible.
  • Avoid covering the leaf with fingers, tools, or background clutter.
  • Prefer one dominant leaf per photo for best accuracy.
  • Avoid extremely distant photos where leaf details cannot be seen.
